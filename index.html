Biography

I am a B.A. (Hons) Translation student in Cross-Cultural Studies & Corporate Communication at Lingnan University, with minors in Economics and Political Science. My research interests centre on individualism–collectivism, cultural linguistics, cultural products, and empirical linguistics.

Since January 2025, I have worked as a Research Assistant at the Comparative Culturology Lab under Professor Plamen Akaliyski, affiliated with Lingnan University. I build and harmonize survey, regional, historical, environmental, and linguistic datasets, and contribute to statistical analysis, manuscripts, and reproducible research workflows.

I am currently an exchange student in the Department of Sociology at National Taiwan University, Taipei, from September to December 2026.

Research orientation

My work combines survey data, corpora, geospatial indicators, remote sensing, historical records, and statistical modelling. I am interested in translating theoretical claims about culture and language into transparent, testable measures.

My experience includes index construction, regression analysis, geospatial matching, robustness checks, and research visualisation, as well as research assistance for an article in the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

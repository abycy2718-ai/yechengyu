Biography

I am YE Chengyu (Allen), originally from Chengdu, China. My academic journey is defined by a curiosity for how language functions as a bridge between cultures and how it can be analyzed through quantitative and empirical lenses.

Currently based in Hong Kong, I balance my undergraduate studies at Lingnan University with active research roles, focusing on the application of economic models and statistical laws to linguistic phenomena.
